Privacy Policy — SwiftHarryDM

Last updated: October 2025

1. Introduction
   Harsh Chaudhary ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it is used.

2. Data We Collect
   a. Required for Licensing and Trial:
      - Machine Identifier (hashed hardware ID) — used to bind a license/trial to a machine.
      - License key (when entered) — for verification.
      - Timestamps of activation/verification.
   b. Optional (only with explicit consent):

   c. Not collected:
      - We DO NOT collect personal files, browsing history, passwords, or content of downloads.

3. How We Use Data
   - License verification and anti-fraud checks.

   - Improve product quality via optional telemetry.

4. Data Sharing

5. Data Storage & Retention

6. Security

7. Children
